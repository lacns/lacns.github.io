bio: I am a second year master's student in the Research Master Cognitive Neuroscience at Radboud University, joining the LaCNS group for my intership. I am fascinated by large-scale neural dynamics and how they help organize language and social behaviour. More recently, I've become interested in the interaction of language production and comprehension. I feel privileged to work on a project that combines these interests, by probing the strategies employed for syntactic parsing/ generation during comprehension/ production of spontaneous speech in MEG, using multivariate temporal response functions. Before coming to Nijmegen, I completed a B.Sc. in Psychology at Ruhr-Universität Bochum. During that period, I had the opportunity to join the Language Cycles group at MPI CBS in Leipzig for an internship, which left a lasting impression on my research interests.
